name: Progression scope
description: User-requested boundary for recommendation features in the hypertrophy tracker.
---

The V2 brief explicitly authorizes a modular adaptive rep/load and progression engine. It must consider repeated exposures, effort, recovery and working-set volume; maintaining is valid. Do not add aggressive automatic deload behavior.

**Why:** the user's V2 request superseded the earlier progression deferral, but explicitly excluded a sophisticated or aggressive deload algorithm.

**How to apply:** improve recommendations within the V2 scope, preserve reproducible target snapshots, and avoid treating a single unusual performance or recovery answer as permission to rewrite the program.
